# Voice Roadmap

The Jarvis spec lists a later voice desk, in this order of impact: barge-in on a live audio stream, a spoken wake word, a boot briefing, a tool-activity rail, an audio-reactive reactor, server-side speech recognition, an optional camera greeting, and a home-screen app.

Always-listening on the open internet is intentionally out. Access and the origin allowlist stay. See [[Barge In Roadmap]] and [[Wake Word Roadmap]].
