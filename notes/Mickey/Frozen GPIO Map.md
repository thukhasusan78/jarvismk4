# Frozen GPIO Map

The assembled Mickey is wired to the Otto no-camera map in `main/boards/mickey/config.h`. Those pins are frozen. Do not edit them to match a camera-Otto sketch.

An older camera-Otto list (legs on 43 and 5, feet on 44 and 6, hands on 4 and 7) is a different starting profile. Flashing that list onto this robot hits the microphone and speaker. The live map is in [[Servo Pins]], [[Microphone and Speaker Pins]], [[Display Pins]], and [[Sensor Pins]]. See [[Do Not Remap Pins]].
