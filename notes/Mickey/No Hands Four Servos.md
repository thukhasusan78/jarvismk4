# No Hands Four Servos

This SKU has no hand servos. GPIO 12 is LCD chip-select only. Hand actions such as hands up, hand wave, greeting, and arm swing are removed and must not be called from vision or MCP.

Motion is four servos: right leg, right foot, left leg, left foot. Face GIFs and voice carry the personality. See [[Servo Pins]] and [[Vision Reflex Table]].
