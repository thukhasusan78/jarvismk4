# Low Battery Inhibit

Low-battery motion inhibit is done. Below the NVS value `mickey/low_bat_pct`, default 15 percent, and while not charging, Mickey rejects walk, jump, dance, and fidget. Home and stop still work.

The backlight dims, the face goes sleepy, and a low-battery sound plays on entry and again every 10 minutes. See [[Idle Director]] and [[Sensor Pins]].
